---
title: Configure a Domain
description: Point a domain at your BeaComm deployment and let clients connect to the fastest node.
---

1. In your domain registrar's console, open the DNS settings for the domain.
2. Add DNS records that resolve the domain to the IP addresses of your BeaComm servers.
3. If you run multiple access nodes, point all of them to the same domain.
4. When clients sign in through the domain, they automatically resolve every node, measure latency, and connect to the fastest one.
