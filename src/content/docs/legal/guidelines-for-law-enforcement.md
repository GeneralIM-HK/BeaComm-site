*Effective date: November 1, 2026*

These guidelines explain what **Hong Kong General IM Technology Limited** and any of its affiliated entities ("BeaComm Team", "we", "us") can and cannot provide to law enforcement authorities in connection with the **BeaComm** software, and how to submit a valid request. These guidelines are informational and do not create any contractual or legal obligation.

## 1. Key point: BeaComm Team holds no user data

BeaComm is **self-hosted software**. We license it to our customers — companies and organizations — who deploy it on servers under their own control. We do not operate any server, provide hosting, or offer any managed service.

As a consequence:

- **We have no access to customer servers.** We cannot access, view, export, or preserve any user data, messages, media, account information, or logs on any server running BeaComm.
- **We hold no end-user data of any kind.** We do not know who uses a customer's server, when, from where, or to talk to whom.
- Conversations on a BeaComm deployment are **end-to-end encrypted** between devices where that feature is enabled. The content of such conversations cannot be read even by the administrators of the server on which they are stored — and therefore not by us.

## 2. Where to direct your request

If your investigation concerns a server running BeaComm, the data you are looking for is in the hands of the organization that operates that server. Please direct your request to:

- **the operator of the server** — the company, organization, or individual that runs the service your subject connects to; or
- **the hosting provider or internet service provider** that hosts or connects the server, who may hold infrastructure-level records (connection logs, billing, IP assignment) under their own legal obligations.

BeaComm Team cannot accept requests for, or act on, data stored on customer-operated servers: there is nothing for us to produce, preserve, or search.

## 3. What BeaComm Team may hold, and when we may disclose it

The only personal data BeaComm Team may hold is limited business information about its own **customers** (licensees) — for example, a business contact name, business email address, company name, and billing records — and correspondence and website access data described in our Privacy Policy.

We disclose such data **only** in response to valid legal process issued under applicable law, such as:

- a subpoena or other lawful demand issued with jurisdiction over BeaComm Team (for example, for basic customer or subscriber information);
- a court order;
- a search warrant, where required for content-related demands.

As a company incorporated in Hong Kong, we assess requests primarily under Hong Kong law — including the disclosure exemptions of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) and the interception-of-communications framework applicable in Hong Kong — and we disclose only what is permitted or required thereunder. For authorities outside Hong Kong, requests should generally be transmitted through international cooperation mechanisms such as mutual legal assistance (MLAT) or letters rogatory; we will assess whether a request has jurisdiction over BeaComm Team and complies with applicable law. We do not respond to informal or voluntary demands for user content, and we do not provide user content at all, because we hold none.

## 4. Emergency situations

If you are investigating an imminent threat to life or of serious injury, contact the operator of the relevant server and your local emergency services **immediately** — do not wait for a response from us.

We will process any emergency disclosure request to the extent permitted by law, but you should expect that we **cannot produce any user content or account data**, because we do not have it. Send emergency requests, clearly marked as such, to the address in Section 7, including: the identity of the person at risk, the nature of the emergency, and the server concerned.

## 5. Preservation requests

We cannot preserve messages, files, account data, or logs on customer servers — those are outside our systems and control. If preservation is needed, it must be requested from the server operator or its hosting provider.

We may, at our discretion or upon a valid preservation request, preserve the limited customer-business data described in Section 3 that we actually hold, for a reasonable period necessary to allow the requesting authority to complete its process. We cannot preserve anything stored on customer servers — those systems and records are entirely outside our reach.

## 6. Notice to customers

Unless legally prohibited — for example, by a lawful gag order or in an emergency involving danger to life — we will notify the affected customer before disclosing their business data, so that they may seek appropriate legal relief.
