*Effective date: November 1, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **Hong Kong General IM Technology Limited** and any of its affiliated entities ("BeaComm Team", "we", "us", "our") handle personal data in connection with the **BeaComm** software and the **BeaComm App**, and our website https://www.beacomm.chat.

BeaComm Team is the developer of the software. The starting point of this policy is the architecture of our product: **we ship a software package that our customers deploy on their own servers. We never receive your conversations.**

## 1. The short version

- Your messages, calls, files, and account data live on the server you connect to — a server operated by our customer, not by us. **We never receive, transit, store, or process them.**
- Conversations protected by end-to-end encryption cannot be read by anyone except the participants — not by the server operator, and not by us.
- The BeaComm App contains **no telemetry**. It sends your messages only to the server you connect to; the only data it may send to us is a sanitized crash report when the App crashes (see Section 4.5).
- What we do process is a short, ordinary list: website access data, correspondence you send us, and the contract and billing records of our business customers.

## 2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the personal data that **BeaComm Team** processes directly.

This policy does **not** cover the processing of personal data carried out on a customer's self-hosted server. If you are an end user connecting to a BeaComm server, the operator of that server ("Server Operator") is the **data controller** of your account and communication data. Please consult the Server Operator's privacy policy to learn what it processes and on what legal basis. BeaComm Team acts as neither controller nor processor for that data.

## 3. Data we never receive

Because of how BeaComm works, the following data never reaches BeaComm Team's systems:

- message, call, and file content exchanged between users of a customer's server;
- user account data (usernames, profile information, credentials) created on a customer's server;
- server logs, IP addresses of end users, or connection metadata recorded by a customer's server;
- any backup or export of a customer's server.

The App communicates only with (a) the server you connect to, (b) the app store or update source it was installed from, and (c) our crash-reporting endpoint, which receives sanitized crash reports as described in Section 4.5. There is no telemetry or analytics that transmits user content or user activity to us. We have designed the product so that we cannot access your conversations, and we do not maintain backdoors of any kind.

## 4. Data we do process

**4.1. Website access data.** When you visit https://www.beacomm.chat, our hosting infrastructure (currently Cloudflare) processes request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamps, for security, abuse prevention, and availability purposes. This data is retained for a short period per the provider's standard configuration. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) / necessary for our operations.

**4.2. Correspondence and support requests.** When you email us or contact us through our website, we process the information you provide (typically your name, email address, and the content of your message) to respond and handle your request. Legal basis: legitimate interests or performance of a contract. Retention: as long as needed to handle the matter and any follow-up, then deleted or anonymized.

**4.3. Customer contract and billing data.** When an organization purchases a BeaComm license, we process the business contact's name, business email address, company name, and billing records (orders, invoices, payment status) to administer the contract, provide support, and comply with accounting and tax obligations. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations. Retention: for the term of the contract and up to seven (7) years thereafter where required for accounting and tax records under Hong Kong law.

**4.4. App distribution.** Downloads and updates of the BeaComm App through the Apple App Store or Google Play are subject to the respective store operator's privacy policy. BeaComm Team does not receive data about your device from the stores beyond aggregated download statistics. For App packages downloaded directly from our website, the website access-data processing described in Section 4.1 applies to the download itself.

**4.5. Crash reports.** When the App crashes, it may automatically submit a sanitized crash report to us so that we can diagnose and fix the underlying problem. Crash reports contain technical information such as the crash log or stack trace, the device and operating system model, and the App version. They are sanitized and do not include your messages, contacts, or other personal content, and they are not used to identify you or to track your behaviour. Crash reporting is part of the App's standard operation. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: for as long as needed to diagnose and fix the issue, then deleted.

## 5. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics. If this changes, we will update this policy before doing so.

## 6. Data sharing

We share personal data only with:

- **infrastructure providers** (currently Cloudflare) acting as processors for our website, under contractual confidentiality and data-processing terms;
- **professional advisers and authorities**, where required by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims (see also our Guidelines for Law Enforcement);
- a successor entity in the event of a merger or sale of our business, under the same protections as this policy.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 7. International transfers

Our website and infrastructure providers may process data in jurisdictions outside your own, including Hong Kong SAR and the United States. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Our current processing of personal data relating to individuals in the EU/EEA is occasional and limited in scale (website access, correspondence, and crash reports), and therefore does not require the designation of an EU representative under Article 27 GDPR; we will reassess this as our business develops.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including the GDPR for individuals in the EU/EEA, the UK GDPR, and Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance), you may request:

- access to the personal data we hold about you;
- rectification of inaccurate data;
- erasure of data we no longer need;
- restriction of or objection to certain processing;
- portability of data you provided to us on the basis of consent or contract.

To exercise these rights, email us (see Section 12). We may need to verify your identity. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may complain to your local supervisory authority — for Hong Kong, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD).

**Important:** requests about your account, messages, or other data held on a BeaComm server must be sent to the **Server Operator**, not to us — we do not hold that data.

## 9. Children

BeaComm is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age applicable in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The minimum age for using the service of a Server Operator is determined by that operator. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

We protect the limited data we hold with technical and organizational measures appropriate to its sensitivity, including encrypted transport (TLS), access controls, and minimization. If a personal-data breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you and the competent authorities as required by law.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product or our processing changes. Material changes will be announced on this page (and, where appropriate, by notice) before they take effect. Previous versions are available on request.
